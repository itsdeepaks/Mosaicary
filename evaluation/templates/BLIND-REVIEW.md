# Blind review packet instructions

Give the reviewer only the original `brief.md`, the required states/viewports, and two unlabeled implementation folders called `A` and `B`.

Do not reveal which condition used `ui-plan` until the reviewer has completed `review.yaml`.

Score each criterion from 1–5, with written evidence for every score that is not 3. Choose `A`, `B`, `tie`, or `not-scored` for `overall_preference`.

Planning review is separate: score the frozen UI Contract against its task rubric before judging the rendered implementation. Do not let a stronger implementation retroactively raise the contract score.
