## Goal

Implement one isolated, non-production Online Scope Studio homepage candidate from the committed Slice 5.1 brief and `tessli.board-research-pack.v1` handoff, retain the first candidate and implementation evidence, and stop before human scoring.

## Route boundary

```text
/proofs/oss-homepage
```

The route is a proof/evaluation surface only:

- not linked from public navigation;
- not included in the sitemap;
- `noindex, nofollow`;
- does not mutate or deploy `https://www.onlinescope.in/`;
- uses no external service, form submission, analytics, CMS, authentication, or private data.

## Acceptance criteria

- one complete candidate implements the committed brief and research-pack constraints;
- the candidate contains a short technical-partner hero, truthful service paths, partnership process, selected-work evidence, capability depth, objection handling, and final CTA;
- primary consultation and secondary work actions are present but inert/non-submitting inside the proof route;
- no unsupported metrics, testimonials, client logos, outcomes, awards, urgency, or social proof are introduced;
- OSS is presented as a studio/technical partner, not as a generic agency or public solo-founder business;
- social-media marketing is not presented as a service;
- the initial 3D hypothesis is explicitly rejected in favour of a bounded CSS system map, with rationale retained;
- selected-source decisions are traceable in implementation evidence without copying provider layouts/assets;
- rejected sources remain excluded unless a documented deviation is recorded;
- the first candidate is retained before review-driven fixes;
- browser output is captured or structurally verified at 1440, 1024, 768, 390, and 320 pixels;
- semantic landmarks, heading order, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, contrast, touch targets, and overflow are checked;
- the proof route remains isolated from Tessli header/footer through route-scoped presentation only;
- implementation start/end, handoff size, approximate token count, changed files, rationale, deviations, and first-candidate state are recorded;
- no human scores or outcome-improvement claim are recorded in Slice 5.2;
- focused structural/browser tests and full exact-head CI/release gates pass;
- Slice 5.2 becomes DONE and Slice 5.3 becomes NEXT only after final validation.

## Candidate decisions

- Provisional accent: restrained electric blue on black/warm-white/grey, because it better separates OSS from Tessli's orange and supports the technical-partner tone. This remains subject to human review.
- Pricing: omitted from this candidate because the homepage pricing decision remains unresolved.
- 3D: rejected for the first candidate; use a CSS system map to express connected services with lower performance and accessibility risk.
- Portfolio evidence: use ScopeQR, Daddy Official, and BrandScope as named work only; do not invent results or metrics.
- Typography: interface-led sans hierarchy with one restrained editorial display treatment; do not copy Tessli's marketing identity wholesale.
