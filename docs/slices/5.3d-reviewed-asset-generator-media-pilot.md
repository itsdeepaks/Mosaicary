## Goal

Run Tessli's explicit media discovery workflow for exactly two low-risk asset-generator resources, inspect every discovered raster manually, and publish only suitable reviewed records through the approved media sidecar.

Pilot resources:

- fffuel — `resource-7fa85ad18d65`
- Haikei — `resource-6887c11205e8`

## Acceptance criteria

1. Discovery runs only for the two listed resource IDs with explicit checked date `2026-08-01`.
2. Network discovery output is captured as a temporary workflow artifact and never committed under `artifacts/`.
3. Every redirect, source-page response, candidate URL, content type, missing preview, failure, or uncertainty is recorded truthfully.
4. No image binary is committed.
5. No authentication, anti-bot protection, paywall, or private content is bypassed.
6. Candidate records remain separate from approved production records until manual review is complete.
7. Every discovered preview and favicon is manually inspected for source-page provenance, raster response type, visual suitability, and obvious advertising or stale-campaign risk.
8. At most two records are approved; unsuitable or unresolved results remain review-only.
9. Any approved record uses an existing resource ID, HTTPS credential-free public raster URLs, verified content types, source page, source type, checked date, and reviewer evidence required by the approved schema.
10. Candidate reports and catalogue outputs are regenerated deterministically from repository sources.
11. Existing fallback order remains manual preview → Open Graph preview → favicon → generated letter mark.
12. Normal build, tests, catalogue generation, and CI remain network-free.
13. No work is performed on Figma Community, UI8, or Creative Market.
14. No UI, auth, Supabase, intelligence-profile, collection, pricing, access-model, dependency, or unrelated catalogue-content change is included.
15. Existing search, save/remove, media fallback, route, runtime-error, responsive, and overflow checks pass at 1440, 1024, 768, 390, and 320px.
16. The final PR contains no temporary discovery workflow or network artifact.
17. `build-slices.md` records 5.3d as `DONE` only after exact-head checks and squash-merge readiness.

## Exclusions

- no discovery for Figma Community, UI8, or Creative Market;
- no mass enrichment of the 295-resource catalogue;
- no screenshot service or automated page capture;
- no image proxy, first-party cache, wildcard image optimiser, or remote SVG;
- no automatic approval or publication;
- no invented URL, MIME type, licence, pricing, access, or rights claim;
- no third-party image binary in Git;
- no changes to card layout or fallback implementation;
- no scheduler or broad refresh system.
