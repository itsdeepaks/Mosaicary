# Product Plan v2 — Phase 5 / Slice 5.3

Status: **IN PROGRESS**

## Goal

Create the bounded, browser-local review workflow for the retained Online Scope Studio homepage candidate, preserve the approved twelve-dimension rubric and browser evidence, and collect genuine human judgment without uploading review data or inventing scores.

## Route boundary

```text
/proofs/oss-homepage/review
```

The route is a proof/evaluation surface only:

- not linked from Tessli public navigation;
- not included in the sitemap;
- `noindex, nofollow`;
- uses no account, backend, analytics, remote storage, email, or external submission;
- stores only a review draft in the current browser;
- exports one explicit versioned JSON review artifact for the reviewer to copy or download;
- does not alter the candidate until a genuine completed review is supplied.

## Acceptance criteria

- the approved twelve review dimensions are represented exactly once and in canonical order;
- every dimension exposes a 1–5 score with clear anchors and requires an evidence note;
- the reviewer records one overall decision: `ship`, `revise`, or `reject`;
- overall notes are required;
- reviewer label and review date remain explicit and editable;
- validation rejects missing scores, out-of-range scores, blank evidence notes, missing decision, invalid date, and blank overall notes;
- a completed artifact uses contract `tessli.oss-homepage-human-review.v1`;
- artifact output is deterministic for the same completed form values;
- Copy JSON and Download JSON use identical bytes;
- the browser-local draft survives refresh and degrades safely when malformed;
- Clear draft requires explicit confirmation and does not affect the candidate or any other Tessli storage key;
- the candidate and current automated evidence are linked without implying that automation is human judgment;
- blind review is explicitly not used because this is a single retained candidate rather than an anonymous A/B comparison;
- no human score is prefilled, inferred, generated, or committed by the implementation agent;
- browser checks cover keyboard operation, labels, validation announcements, local-only messaging, copy/download readiness, responsive overflow, and route isolation;
- the route remains absent from public navigation and sitemap;
- no candidate code, live OSS site, source profile, dependency, provider, credential, or deployment configuration changes;
- safe repository work may become `BLOCKED` after merge until a genuine reviewer completes and returns the exported artifact;
- Slice 5.4 remains blocked until the completed review artifact and any approved review-driven corrections are recorded.

## Canonical dimensions

1. task fit;
2. hierarchy;
3. mobile usability;
4. discoverability;
5. density;
6. coherence;
7. consistency;
8. component reuse;
9. accessibility;
10. restraint;
11. regression risk;
12. ship readiness.

## Expected files

- versioned review contract and validator;
- browser-local review form and storage helper;
- isolated review route and CSS;
- Markdown reviewer packet and empty JSON example/template;
- focused contract/storage/route/browser tests;
- CI/release evidence hooks for the review route;
- ledger and slice evidence updates after exact-head validation.

## Exclusions

- no AI-generated or inferred human score;
- no completion of the review on behalf of the owner;
- no review-driven candidate change before a genuine artifact exists;
- no candidate redesign, production OSS mutation, or Tessli public navigation change;
- no cloud storage, authentication, sharing, moderation, database, analytics, email, or external form submission;
- no Pattern Candidate promotion, outcome report, or UI-taste claim;
- no dependency, Supabase, credential, provider, or deployment-state change.

## Rollback

Remove the isolated review route, review contract/storage/UI, reviewer packet/template, focused tests, CI evidence hooks, and completion-ledger references. No candidate, live OSS site, public Tessli route, provider, credential, or deployment state requires rollback.
