## 1. Project Brief

### Goal

Reposition the current Online Scope Studio homepage from a broad website-development offer into a clear, credible long-term technical-partner story for small-to-medium businesses, while preserving truthful services and an understandable conversion path.

### Audience

Small-to-medium business owners, founders, and marketing or operations leaders who need one accountable technical partner across websites, commerce, custom web applications, automation, measurement, maintenance, and UI/UX.

### Constraints

Use a black, warm-white, and grey foundation with one restrained signal accent. Keep the hero short and balanced. Show business outcomes before tool logos. Preserve real project evidence only. Do not present OSS as a generic agency or public solo-founder business. Do not add social-media marketing. Avoid generic AI-purple styling, glassmorphism, cursor effects, scroll-jacking, fake proof, and decorative 3D. Any motion or 3D must improve comprehension and remain accessible, responsive, and performance-conscious.
