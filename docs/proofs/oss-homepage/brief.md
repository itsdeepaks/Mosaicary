## Observed current-site baseline

On 2026-08-04, the public homepage:

- used the title `Website Development for Small Business India | Online Scope Studio`;
- led with `We Build Websites & Systems That Grow With Your Business`;
- addressed Indian business owners who are new online, selling on marketplaces, or ready to scale;
- presented Shopify Store Development, Business Automation, and Custom Web Applications as the three core services;
- described a four-step process: Discovery Call, Proposal & Planning, Build & Review, Launch & Support;
- highlighted ScopeQR, Daddy Official, and BrandScope;
- used consultation and WhatsApp conversion paths;
- contained audience, expertise, process, selected-work, tools, automation, and CTA sections.

These observations preserve the baseline. They do not endorse every current claim or lock the candidate copy.

## Baseline gap

The current page establishes website and automation capability, but still reads mainly as a website-development offer for small businesses. The proof candidate must test whether Tessli research can communicate a broader, longer-term technical-partner relationship without becoming vague, generic, over-designed, or enterprise theatre.

## Project decisions

### Positioning

- Brand: **Online Scope Studio** / **OSS**.
- Present OSS as a studio and long-term technical partner, not a generic agency and not a public solo-founder business.
- Lead with business outcomes and partnership; tools support the story.
- Keep the service scope grounded in website engineering, e-commerce, custom web applications, automation, AI-enabled workflow additions, analytics/measurement, SEO/maintenance, and UI/UX.
- Do not add social-media marketing.

### Audience

Primary audience:

- small-to-medium business owners and founders;
- marketing and operations leaders who need accountable technical execution;
- businesses that prefer one technical partner across websites, commerce, applications, and automation.

The page must remain clear to non-technical buyers while demonstrating enough system depth to earn technical trust.

### Homepage job

Within the first screen and first two sections, a qualified visitor should understand:

1. what OSS helps businesses build or improve;
2. that OSS can remain involved beyond a one-off website;
3. the main service paths;
4. why the work is credible;
5. the next action.

### Visual direction

- black, warm white, and grey foundation with one restrained signal accent;
- technical and modern, but warmer and more original than a direct OpenAI, Vercel, Resend, or shadcn imitation;
- short, visually balanced hero headline;
- typography, whitespace, borders, and moderate density carry hierarchy;
- restrained motion and at most one meaningful 3D/technical visual hypothesis;
- real work and interface evidence before decorative imagery;
- no generic AI-purple palette, glassmorphism, cursor-follow effects, scroll-jacking, decorative orbs, or meaningless floating 3D;
- no fake metrics, testimonials, client logos, awards, urgency, or social proof.

### Interaction and conversion

- responsive at 1440, 1024, 768, 390, and 320 pixels;
- semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, and touch-safe targets;
- mobile is recomposed, not merely shrunk;
- one primary consultation/project-start action and one secondary proof/work action;
- WhatsApp may remain an option but must not compete with every section;
- no dead newsletter, sign-in, or placeholder action.

## Candidate architecture hypothesis

1. Short positioning hero.
2. Proof of technical range through selected work or system examples.
3. Three to four outcome-oriented service paths.
4. How the partnership works.
5. Capability depth without a tool-logo wall.
6. Selected work with truthful context.
7. Objection handling and final CTA.

This is a research hypothesis, not a layout to copy.

## Open questions

- Should the signal accent remain warm orange or move toward restrained blue?
- Should fixed starting prices appear on the homepage or remain on a dedicated pricing page?
- Which three to four projects have publishable outcomes beyond screenshots?
- Does one meaningful 3D/technical visual improve comprehension enough to justify its cost?
- Should the audience remain broad across Indian SMBs or narrow toward operations-heavy growth businesses?
- Which short phrase communicates long-term technical partnership without outsourced-CTO theatre?
